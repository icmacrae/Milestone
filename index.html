About Our College

Your Next Milestone Starts Here

Milestone College is a proposed computer training institution based in Brossard, Quebec, Canada. Our vision is to provide accessible IT education for individuals looking to build or improve their technology skills.

From entry-level computer support to server and network administration, our proposed programs cover different areas of information technology.

  • Computer and technical support
  • Server and network administration
  • Microsoft technologies
  • Cloud and cybersecurity fundamentals
